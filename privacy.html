PocketRehab privacy policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

PocketRehab is a free app that helps you follow a home rehab plan. It is built so your health information stays with you.

What stays on your device

  • Your plan, sessions, check-ins and measurements are stored in your browser on your device, encrypted (with a key from your password, or a device key for Google accounts).
  • Camera video is processed on your device to estimate joint angles. It is never recorded, stored or uploaded.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared.

Optional Google sign-in

If you choose to connect a Google account, PocketRehab uses it to back up and restore your data:

  • Your name and email are used only to show which Google account is connected.
  • Backups are saved in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission) that other apps and people cannot see. PocketRehab cannot read your other Drive files. Backups made from a password account are also encrypted with that password before upload.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings, and remove the backup from your Google Drive settings under "Manage apps".

Deleting your data

Delete your account in Settings to erase its data from this device. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data also removes it.

Not medical advice

PocketRehab supports a plan from or approved by a qualified professional. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, and its camera measurements can be inaccurate.
